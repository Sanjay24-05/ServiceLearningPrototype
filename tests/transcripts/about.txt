heading level 1: How Slide Proof works
text: Slide Proof checks how your slides will look to a sighted audience and tells you, in words, what a sighted person would notice. It can also fix some problems for you, on a copy.
heading level 2: What it checks
text: Objects that stick out past the slide edge
text: Stretched pictures
text: Overlapping objects
text: Low-resolution pictures
text: Titles out of line with the rest
text: Stray fonts
text: Empty or stray objects
text: Not checked yet: text overflow, contrast and text size.
heading level 2: How fixes work
text: Fixes are only applied to a copy of your file, never the original. After fixing, Slide Proof checks the copy again and undoes any fix that did not work or caused a new problem.
heading level 2: Your privacy
text: Your file stays on this computer. Nothing is uploaded to the internet, and files from earlier sessions are deleted each time Slide Proof starts.
heading level 2: What it cannot do yet
text: It does not check text overflow, contrast or text size yet.
text: It does not look inside charts and diagrams.
text: Its judgement of overlap uses an estimate of where text sits; a later version will use the rendered slide.
