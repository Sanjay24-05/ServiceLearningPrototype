How Slide Proof works

Slide Proof checks how your slides will look to a sighted audience and tells you, in words, what a sighted person would notice. It can also fix some problems for you, on a copy.

How fixes work

Fixes are only applied to a copy of your file, never the original. After fixing, Slide Proof checks the copy again and undoes any fix that did not work or caused a new problem.

Your privacy

Your file stays on this computer. Nothing is uploaded to the internet, and files from earlier sessions are deleted each time Slide Proof starts.
