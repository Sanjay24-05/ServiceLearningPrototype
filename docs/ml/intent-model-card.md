# Intent model card

All training data is synthetic. The decks were generated by `tools/make_training_decks.py` with a fixed seed (7); no real student, teacher or institutional slide was used for training or for choosing the threshold. The held-out check below uses hand-built test decks and one real third-party template (Yellow Idea).

## Purpose

Slide Proof's off-slide (C1) and overlap (C2) checks flag geometry that may be a mistake or a deliberate design choice (full-bleed background, corner logo, caption band, frame). The model estimates the probability that a candidate is deliberate. Findings with probability at or above the threshold may be moved out of the main list; everything else stays. A wrong move demotes a real mistake to the Probably intentional section, with its fix unticked, so **mistake recall wins over usefulness**: the threshold is chosen for mistake recall first.

## Patterns and ranges

120 decks, each with 2-4 deliberate patterns (`deliberate-<pattern>-<n>`) and 2-4 mistakes (`mistake-<pattern>-<n>`) placed from overlapping position, size and colour ranges, with variants per pattern so no single feature separates the classes. Labels come from element-name prefixes only. A candidate with names from both prefixes, or from neither, is dropped. The model sees 21 geometric and structural features (see `features.json`), never names or text.

## Class counts

Rows per (deck, pattern instance) are capped at 3 (seeded) so repeated logos, footers and text-panel highlights do not dominate.

| | Deliberate | Mistake | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Before cap | 874 | 827 | 1701 |
| After cap | 611 | 706 | 1317 |
| After cap, judged by the app | 529 | 593 | 1122 |

The model trains on every row after the cap. Text that sticks out (C1 on a title, body or text box, or on any element with text) is never judged by the app, so the threshold and the metrics below use only the rows the app would judge.

Dropped before labelling: 29 mixed-prefix, 0 content-only candidates.

## Metrics

Five-fold GroupKFold (grouped by deck), out-of-fold probabilities, at each family's own threshold. The threshold and the model family are chosen on the same out-of-fold predictions that are reported here, so the numbers are mildly optimistic. Confusion is TP / FP / FN / TN with "deliberate" as the positive class (FP = a mistake that would be moved).

| Family | Threshold | Mistake recall | Deliberate recall | Precision | F1 | Confusion |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Logistic regression | 0.87 | 0.951 | 0.594 | 0.915 | 0.720 | 314 / 29 / 215 / 564 |
| Gradient boosting | 0.80 | 0.976 | 0.943 | 0.973 | 0.958 | 499 / 14 / 30 / 579 |

Shipped: **Gradient boosting** (logistic regression unless gradient boosting F1 is more than 0.05 higher).

## Threshold

The smallest t >= 0.80 on a 0.01 grid where out-of-fold mistake recall is at least 0.95. The safety target (mistake recall of at least 0.95) was met at t = 0.80. Shipped threshold: 0.80.

## Held-out decks (not used in training)

The shipped model is applied to exactly the findings the app judges: C1 findings on elements without text (not a title, body or text box), and C2 findings whose upper element is a picture or filled shape. The demo deck and the test corpus are hand-built test decks; only Yellow Idea is a real third-party template.

| Deck set | Judged | Would move | Planted mistakes | Planted moved |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Demo deck (hand-built test deck) | 1 | 0 | 1 | 0 |
| Test corpus (hand-built test decks) | 1 | 0 | 1 | 0 |
| Yellow Idea (real third-party template) | 5 | 1 | 0 | 0 |

Planted mistakes moved: **0** (must be 0). Yellow Idea: 1 of 5 judged findings would move.

## Known limits

- Untested on real-world decks.
- The training distribution is generated by us, so scores measure agreement with our own generator, not with real designers. Treat them as a sanity check, not a field accuracy.
- The model leans on "the same object on several slides". A mistake copied by duplicating a slide repeats too, so duplicated-slide mistakes (`mistake-dup-<n>`) were added to the generator after review on 2026-11-22, and text that sticks out is never judged.
- Held-out sets are tiny (1 demo, 1 corpus, 5 Yellow Idea judged findings; 2 planted mistakes in total); zero planted mistakes moved is weak evidence, not proof.
- The Yellow Idea move (C1 on slide 8, p = 1.000) has not been confirmed as deliberate by a person; it is unverified.
- Text-over-text overlaps are not judged. Rotated, grouped and placeholder-heavy designs are lightly covered.
- Generator history: variants were added so no single feature separates the classes, and rows per pattern instance are capped.

## How to retrain

```
.venv/Scripts/python tools/train_intent_model.py          # full, 120 decks, seed 7
.venv/Scripts/python tools/train_intent_model.py --quick  # 12 decks, for tests
```

This rewrites `slideproof/intent/model.joblib`, `features.json`, `metrics.json` and this card. scikit-learn is pinned to the version recorded in `features.json`.
