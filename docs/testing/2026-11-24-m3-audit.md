# M3 audit: rendered checks and before/after view (2026-11-24)

Environment: Windows 11, LibreOffice 26.8.3, NVDA 2026.3.1, Microsoft Edge. Code: master after PRs #11 and #12.

## 1. What M3 added

Slide Proof now reads the slides as LibreOffice actually draws them (PDF text spans and PNG pixels), not only the file's geometry.

- **Rendered text.** Each drawn text span is linked to the object it belongs to. Overlap (C2) and hidden-content checks use the area the text really fills.
- **Four new checks:**
  - **Text overflow (C3):** text runs more than 0.1 in past the bottom or right of its box.
  - **Small text (C6):**
    - under 12 pt is a visible problem;
    - under 18 pt in body text is a minor one;
    - footers, dates and slide numbers are exempt.
  - **Low contrast (C7):**
    - the WCAG ratio is measured against the pixels behind each piece of text;
    - confidence is "likely" over photos;
    - footers, dates and slide numbers are minor.
  - **Hidden content (C11):** an object covers 95% or more of another. It is reported once, never also as an overlap, low contrast or small text.
- **Before/after view.** The result page has a "Show the changed slides" section. Each changed slide is shown next to its corrected version. Each picture's description is the list of changes made to that slide.
- **Coverage text.** The report, the About page and the "checks were skipped" note now name all eleven checks.

## 4. Reviews and what they caught

Every task had a spec and quality review, and the whole branch then had a final review, followed by a re-review of the fixes. Notable catches:

- **Autofit boxes.** PowerPoint grows these boxes or shrinks their text, and LibreOffice does neither. Overflow (C3) now skips them, and their rendered text is clipped to the box for overlap. This removed false alarms on the demo agenda box and two long titles.
- **Very faint text.** Very light grey on white was skipped as "unmeasurable". The check now measures a thin ring around the text when the text is too close to its background.
- **Text cut off the slide (final review, critical).** LibreOffice leaves out text that falls past the page edge. Measuring from the drawn text therefore missed or under-reported cut text, for example a box half off the bottom of the slide. Now:
  - the drawn text only clears a box when it stays well inside the slide;
  - otherwise the problem is measured from the box, as in M2;
  - overflow (C3) uses an unclipped estimate at the slide edge.
- **Double reports (final review).** Text hidden under a shape was also reported as low contrast, measured against the covering shape. Hidden text is now reported once.
- **Grouping.** Many minor findings on one slide could be promoted to a visible problem. Groups now stay in their members' tier.

Test suite: 390 tests, including 39 that render with LibreOffice and the axe accessibility tests.

## 5. Known limits (for M4)

- C3 does not catch text that overflows upward in boxes anchored at the bottom or middle.
- See-through overlays and transparent pictures are treated as solid when deciding what is hidden.
- The "text fits on the slide" exemption assumes every piece of a box's text was matched to that box.
- Stray fonts (C9) are still read from the file, not from the rendered text.
- Rendering is LibreOffice's. Fonts, line breaks and autofit can differ from PowerPoint, so overflow and contrast findings are marked "likely" where that matters.
- The before/after pictures are small at the page width. Opening one at full size may help sighted helpers.
